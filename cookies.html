Last updated: 11 October 2026

We keep cookies and similar technologies to a minimum.

Essential storage

We use local storage named nte-cookie-consent to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies. This is necessary to respect your choice and does not track you across websites.

Optional analytics

No analytics or advertising scripts are loaded by default. If analytics are introduced later, they will load only after you choose “Accept optional” in the banner. You can change your choice by clearing this site’s local storage or contacting us.

Third-party links

Amazon and other linked sites may set their own cookies after you leave Nobody Told Me Either. Their policies apply there.
